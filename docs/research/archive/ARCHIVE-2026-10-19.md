## Use of this dataset
The research records are retained for three purposes:
- **Gap analysis:** finding pins and sets that Pin & Pop does not cover.
- **Validation:** cross-checking mapped Pin & Pop records.
- **Possible future contribution:** offering records to Pin & Pop, which needs the maintainer's approval and agreed terms.

Nothing in this archive is imported into Supabase or production.

To re-check the archive: `git ls-remote --tags origin 'archive/2026-10-19/*'` should list the five SHAs above.
