# PinPal is a US marketplace, not a UK rival

PinPal (thepinpal.com, iOS app "PinPal: Pin Trading & Scanner", App Store id6756037215) is a **young, US-centric Disney pin social marketplace** with no visible public traction yet. It launched around mid-2026, still had **no displayable App Store rating** by October, and has no Android app. It also has **no indexed evidence of Disneyland Paris, Disney Store UK, Loungefly or Artland coverage**, GBP/EUR pricing, or UK/EU shipping support ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)). Its real strengths are a public, SEO-indexed web catalogue with per-pin and per-set pages, free photo scanning, iPad support and an in-app marketplace with checkout. That marketplace is a model PinHunt has deliberately ruled out. **The headline "85k+ pins" claim could not be found in any public source.** The larger catalogue figures (130k and 150k) belong to, or were confused with, a different app. PinPal's data cannot legally be imported or used for systematic verification. PinHunt's most urgent gap is internal rather than competitive: its live trusted catalogue (182 pins) has **zero DLP, Loungefly or Artland pins**, and its two monthly 2026 series stop at July. The UK/EU niche is open, but PinHunt has to fill it before the niche counts as an advantage.

> **Method limitation: read before using any PinPal finding externally.** This environment's network policy blocked direct access to thepinpal.com and apps.apple.com: WebFetch failed DNS, and the proxy returned CONNECT 403. **Every PinPal finding comes from search-engine snippets or aggregator summaries of their pages, not from pages opened directly.** About 12 PinPal URLs were indexed in total, so "not found on PinPal" means *not determinable*, not *absent*. No scraping, image downloads, endpoint probing or intrusive testing was done, and robots.txt and the Terms of Service were not read. Spot-check every claim in a normal browser before relying on it. Labels below: **Verified (snippet)** means the text was attributed to an official PinPal or App Store page in a search result. **Assumed** means analyst inference.

## Strengths and weaknesses, verified versus assumed

PinPal's **verified strengths** are distribution and breadth. It runs a **public web catalogue** at `/catalog/pin/{uuid}`, `/catalog/set/{uuid}/{slug}` and `/explore`. Search covers character, origin, set, year and photo, and set pages carry SEO titles such as "Blue Paint Jar Disney Pins" ([Explore](https://thepinpal.com/explore); [set page](https://thepinpal.com/catalog/set/1cf000db-0c8c-4208-910c-a4da68dbd96f/blue-paint-jar)). Pin pages show origin, collection, set, edition size, year, sometimes original retail price, and live **collector / for sale / for trade counts** ([Simba Symbol](https://thepinpal.com/catalog/pin/45b4ac0d-9a25-41f4-a783-ff0bab10a7b3)). The catalogue has historical depth: WDI 2021 LE 300, Disneyland 60th 2015, a 2008 WDI set, DEC 2024 sets. It also has at least one 2026 Hong Kong Disneyland record ([Elsa, Anna & Olaf](https://thepinpal.com/catalog/pin/09512131-07ea-4c4c-ba10-13ca231b2dcf)). The app advertises **unlimited free scans** (moderate confidence), and it added iPad support plus events and trading-location maps in 1.2.0 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)). Its liability wording is careful and worth copying: "A catalog match is a research lead. It does not authenticate the physical pin or establish its value" ([pin page](https://thepinpal.com/catalog/pin/3665a1dd-3c44-4a2a-874f-e186f52c535b)).

Its **verified weaknesses** are traction and focus. There is no displayable rating, no Google Play app, and **no public user discussion anywhere** (Reddit, Disney Pin Forum, Trustpilot, YouTube). There is no automated valuation, only a linked "value research guide" and "asking-price references". The only marketplace price seen was **$10.00**. Site searches for "Disneyland Paris", Pin Trading Night, Loungefly, Artland, Windows of Attraction, Enchanted Doors, Brush of Magic and Animated Cuties returned **zero PinPal pages** ([Explore](https://thepinpal.com/explore)).

**Assumed** (unproven): the catalogue was bulk-seeded from community databases, since its WDI, DEC and MII origin codes mirror PinPics and Pin & Pop conventions. Its sets are very granular, including one-pin "sets", which would inflate counts. Premium gates marketplace or collection extras rather than scans. PinPal takes a sales commission. Its European coverage is thin rather than just poorly indexed.

## Images and licensing: use PinPal only as a lead, never as a source

PinPal's image host, CDN, and image or data provenance are **unknown**. Pages refer to "reference images" and "approved PinPal records", which suggests a moderation queue, but no indexed page names a data partner. No Disney licence or partnership with Pin & Pop or PinPics was found ([set page](https://thepinpal.com/catalog/set/1cf000db-0c8c-4208-910c-a4da68dbd96f/blue-paint-jar)). The ToS and Privacy Policy are linked from the footer, but their text was not indexed ([home](https://thepinpal.com/)). There is **no public API, developer documentation, export or partner programme**, and robots.txt and the sitemap were unreadable. The only stable keys are opaque UUIDs, so any cross-reference would have to match on name, set, year and edition.

The legal position (this is not legal advice) rules out reuse. UK database right is infringed by extracting a substantial part, *including through repeated, systematic extraction of insubstantial parts* ([reg. 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/3032/regulation/16?view=plain); [Art. 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1996/9/article/7)). A US LLC may not qualify for UK or EU sui generis protection, but that does **not** make copying safe. Typical ToS anti-scraping clauses bind as contract, and pin photos carry both Disney artwork copyright and the photographer's copyright. A lawyer should confirm both points.

**Verdict: no direct import of PinPal data or images, and no automated lookups.** PinPal can support PinHunt's research and verification only as a **one-off human research lead**, for example an admin checking one pin to resolve a conflict. That only applies after someone reads the ToS, and the fact must then be corroborated against a rights-cleared source, which becomes the provenance recorded in the Master Catalogue. In `research/catalogue-engine/access_policy.json`, thepinpal.com should be recorded as `network_reachable: false`, `terms_reviewed: false` and `fetch_permitted: false`.

Legitimate routes, in order:

1. Finish the **Collectible PinTrader** rights record. It is already permitted, but the CC variant is still a TODO.
2. Grow **consented user submissions** with an explicit licence grant, using the existing contribute flow.
3. Use **official Disney, DLP and shopDisney UK pages for facts only**.
4. Continue with the **eBay official API**.
5. Pursue **Pin & Pop through the `PinRepository` seam**. It is the natural DLP partner.
6. Send a low-cost **licence enquiry to 7Labs**.

## Five UK and European openings PinPal is not built for

UK and EU collecting is spread across many channels and has a lot of friction. DLP limited editions go through Lineberty queues that open two days ahead at 6pm, with caps per guest, and they sell out within days ([pintrader.club](https://www.pintrader.club/articles/buying-limited-edition-pins-at-disneyland-paris)). Disney Store UK runs **£25 ticketed "Reels of Magic" events**: Manchester on 13 Nov 2026 and London Oxford Street on 25 Nov 2026, with tickets on sale **21 Oct at 5pm** and location exclusives limited to 2 per design ([Mickey From The UK](https://mickeyfromthe.uk/news/disney-store-pin-trading-events-coming-to-london-manchester)). Independent meetups are coordinated by Instagram DM ([Disney and Days Out](https://disneyanddaysout.co.uk/uk-pin-trading-events-2026)). Since **1 July 2026**, UK-to-EU gifts over €45 incur **€3 per HS code plus a €2 handling fee** ([Royal Mail](https://www.royalmail.com/europeantradeinsights)). Counterfeits of a DLP LE 700 series were confirmed in February 2026 ([Disniverse](https://disniverse.com/pintrading/)).

## Conclusion

PinPal shows where the US market is going: scanning is free, catalogues are public and SEO-driven, and monetisation comes from transactions. It does not compete for the UK or Paris collector, and PinHunt's no-payments stance is not a weakness against it. For a swap-only community with no buyer protection, that stance is the basis for a trust-led product. PinPal's real lesson for PinHunt is distribution. Indexed set pages and a free web identifier collect search traffic that PinHunt, sitting behind a login, cannot reach.

The decision that matters is internal. PinHunt's UK/EU advantage exists only on paper until the Master Catalogue holds DLP, Disney Store UK, Loungefly and Artland pins, and until its 2026 series are current. Catalogue completeness in that niche, sourced cleanly, is a stronger move than matching any PinPal feature. Before anyone cites a PinPal fact externally, someone should open thepinpal.com and the App Store listing in an ordinary browser to confirm the catalogue size, payment model, ISO features and ToS.
