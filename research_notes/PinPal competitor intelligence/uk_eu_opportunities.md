Research date: 2026-10-14. Public sources only. Labels: **VERIFIED** = stated in a cited public source (as returned by search; note that direct page fetches were blocked by the session's network policy, so every finding comes from search-result content and summaries, not full-page reads — spot-check key figures before quoting them externally). **ASSUMED** = analyst inference, not directly sourced.

## 1. UK/EU collector pain points (DLP releases, Disney Store UK, GBP/EUR, cross-border shipping, language, meetups)

### Takeaway
UK/EU collectors deal with a release calendar that is fragmented and full of friction: DLP limited editions (LEs) go through the Lineberty virtual queue with per-ticket caps and fast sell-outs, and Disney Store UK runs ticketed £25 trading events with location exclusives. Since 1 July 2026, posting pins from the UK into the EU costs more: once a gift parcel is worth more than €45 it pays per-HS-code duty plus a handling fee, so trades across the Channel cost noticeably more. No single tool collects all of this in GBP/EUR.

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED** — DLP held an *Enchanted*-themed Pin Trading Night at Cowboy Cookout Barbecue, Frontierland, in September 2026. Entry needed Lineberty tickets, with one guest per ticket holder. The sources disagree on the date (17 vs 19 September) — [Laughing Place](https://www.laughingplace.com/disney-parks/dlp-pin-trading-nights-sept-2026/)
- **VERIFIED** — DLP holds a pin collector event "at least once a month" (a big Pin Event, a Pin Trading Day or a Pin Trading Night). Pueblo Trading Post trades on Saturdays and Sundays from 10am, and that schedule "is subject to change at any moment" — [dein-dlrp.de guide](https://www.dein-dlrp.de/en/disneyland-paris-holiday/disneyana-collectibles/pin-trading.html)
- **VERIFIED** — DLP LE pin releases are reserved through the Lineberty app. Digital tickets open two days before the sale at 6pm. One ticket covers one person and allows two pins per release — [WDWNT, March 2019 DLP releases](https://wdwnt.com/2019/03/march-2019-pin-releases-for-disneyland-paris/); [Disney Pin Forum Lineberty trip report](https://www.disneypinforum.com/threads/lineberty-at-dlp-a-trip-report.77612/); [pintrader.club](https://www.pintrader.club/articles/buying-limited-edition-pins-at-disneyland-paris); [Laughing Place, March 2025](https://www.laughingplace.com/w/disney-parks/disneyland-paris-pin-release-march-2025/)
- **VERIFIED (anecdotal)** — DLP LEs "tend to sell out within a couple of days at most". Users say Lineberty can be hard to connect to and you have to keep retrying when the queue opens — [Tripadvisor DLP forum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g2079053-i21935-k12469704-Limited_edition_pins_Lineberty-Disneyland_Paris.html); [pintrader.club](https://www.pintrader.club/articles/buying-limited-edition-pins-at-disneyland-paris)
- **VERIFIED** — DLP LE pins resell well above retail. One example is a 2025 Stitch LE 573/900 that sold for US$102 on eBay. This is a single sale, not a trend — [search summary of eBay listings](https://www.ebay.com/itm/325853960713)
- **VERIFIED** — Disney Store UK "Reels of Magic" ticketed pin trading events: Selfridges/Trafford Centre, Manchester on 13 Nov 2026 and the Oxford Street flagship in London on 25 Nov 2026. Tickets cost £25 and go on sale at 5pm on 21 Oct 2026. Manchester has 4 location exclusives and London has 5, plus an event-exclusive pin, an LE pin set and an event pin board. Limit of 2 per design per guest. London runs in 1-hour slots and Manchester in 45-minute slots — [Mickey From The UK](https://mickeyfromthe.uk/news/disney-store-pin-trading-events-coming-to-london-manchester)
- **VERIFIED** — Earlier 2026 Disney Store "Tails of Friendship" events: Oxford Street, London (1 July) and Grafton Street, Dublin (8 July), with tickets released 3 June — [Disney Pins Blog](https://disneypinsblog.com/tails-of-friendship-disney-pin-trading-event/); [Disney and Days Out](https://disneyanddaysout.co.uk/uk-pin-trading-events-2026)
- **VERIFIED** — The UK has independent meetups. A community calendar lists monthly pin trading days (for example Get Ready Comics, Rochester, usually the last Sunday of the month, with some dates "TBD"), Pinsane events (Runcorn 10–11 Oct 2026, and the Annual London Pin Trading Event at Delta Hotels Heathrow Windsor, 18–19 Jul 2026). The calendar is kept up by Instagram DM submissions — [Disney and Days Out](https://disneyanddaysout.co.uk/uk-pin-trading-events-2026); [Pinsane](https://www.pinsane.co.uk/event-details/pinsane-pins-pin-trading-event)
- **VERIFIED** — Royal Mail requires a CN22 for goods or gifts under £270 and a CN23 above that. Some third-party guides give £150, so the sources conflict — [Post Office customs forms](https://www.postoffice.co.uk/mail/customs-forms); [postofficehours.co.uk](https://postofficehours.co.uk/blog/cn22-cn23-customs-forms-guide)
- **VERIFIED (needs re-check against an official EU source)** — Royal Mail's EU trade page says private-to-private gifts worth €45 or less pay no extra duty. Above €45 there is a €3 duty per HS code from 1 Jul 2026 plus a €2 handling fee, collected on delivery. A flat €3 duty on B2C consignments of €150 or less also started on 1 Jul 2026 — [Royal Mail European Trade Insights](https://www.royalmail.com/europeantradeinsights)
- **VERIFIED** — Guides warn that vague descriptions such as "Gift" or "personal items" trigger inspection, that a fair market value (not zero) should be declared, and that recipients may have to pay a fee before collecting — [dutiable.io CN22 guide](https://dutiable.io/blog/how-to-file-cn22-customs-declaration); [International Logistics Group](https://www.international-logistics-group.com/us-en/insights/cn22-cn23-customs-forms-guide-161649/); [Royal Mail pay customs fees](https://help.royalmail.com/personal/s/article/Pay-customs-fees)
- **VERIFIED** — Official UK pin badges typically sell for £10–£30, with wide variation on the secondary market — [Collectors Club of Great Britain](https://www.collectors-club-of-great-britain.co.uk/collecting/the-ultimate-guide-to-disney-pin-trading/)

### Inferences
- **ASSUMED** — The DLP + Disney Store UK + independent meetup calendar is spread across Lineberty, Instagram, fan blogs and DM-maintained lists. A single **"UK/EU Release & Event Radar"** (DLP Pin Trading Nights and Days, Lineberty LE drop times, Disney Store UK ticket-release times, UK meetups) with ISO-linked alerts ("an LE on your ISO list releases Saturday, Lineberty opens Thursday 6pm") is a clear gap that US-centric apps are unlikely to fill well.
- **ASSUMED** — Location exclusives at Disney Store UK events and the 2-per-guest caps at DLP and Disney Store create built-in trade demand: attendees end up holding duplicates or event exclusives that non-attendees want. "Event trade boards" (who's attending, what they can pick up, what they want in return) fit PinHunt's trade-matching and ISO/For Trade roadmap.
- **ASSUMED** — Because of the 1 Jul 2026 EU fee change, a UK↔EU trade worth more than €45 per side now carries a likely €5+ cost for the recipient, plus CN22 paperwork. A **trade-shipping helper** (keeping with PinHunt's rule that each collector buys their own postage) would reduce friction: a cross-border flag, a per-HS-code fee estimate, CN22 description templates ("enamel lapel pin", declared value from the PinHunt valuation) and a "keep under €45" nudge. US apps have no reason to build this.
- **ASSUMED** — Valuation in GBP and EUR, drawn from eBay UK, eBay.de/.fr and Vinted, is a differentiator. US apps price in USD from eBay.com.

## 2. How UK/EU collectors currently trade and catalogue, and community size

### Takeaway
UK/EU trading runs on in-person events (DLP, Disney Store UK, independent meetups), Instagram and DMs, eBay (.co.uk/.de) and Vinted, with Pin & Pop/PinPics used as reference catalogues. I could not verify any public member counts for Facebook, Reddit or Discord communities.

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED** — Vinted UK has many Disney pin listings, including DLP "Official Pin Trading" pins. Vinted's listing pages say statutory consumer rights (right to cancel or reject) do not apply to purchases from private individuals. Vinted Buyer Protection covers only purchases made with "Buy now" — [Vinted UK listing](https://www.vinted.co.uk/items/1374593576-pins-disney-official-pin-trading-panpan); [Vinted UK listing](https://www.vinted.co.uk/items/481175479-pins-disney)
- **VERIFIED (third-party)** — Vinted sellers pay no fee; buyers pay a Buyer Protection fee (US example: $0.70 + 5%). The UK rate was given only as "usually 3% to 8%" and is not confirmed — [underpriced.app](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/vinted-fees-2026)
- **VERIFIED** — A large secondary market for DLP pins sits on eBay.de (for example "Pin Trading Time Pacha LE 400", "Pin Trading Night 2016 LE 400", Tangled LE 400). Listings often quote PinPics numbers as identifiers — [eBay.de example](https://www.ebay.de/itm/187291013502); [eBay.de example](https://www.ebay.de/itm/335376951243); [eBay.de example](https://www.ebay.de/itm/186238179615)
- **VERIFIED** — Instagram is the coordination channel for UK events: Disney and Days Out takes event submissions by Instagram DM, and its guidance points to shop Instagrams for dates — [Disney and Days Out](https://disneyanddaysout.co.uk/uk-pin-trading-events-2026)
- **VERIFIED** — A UK seller ("PinPalUK", pin-pal.com) trades by social-media DMs and lists prices in GBP/EUR/USD/JPY. It is **not** confirmed to be linked to the PinPal app — [pin-pal.com](https://pin-pal.com/pages/pin-trading)
- **VERIFIED** — Disney Pin Forum has a "Gatherings, Events & Meetups" section and UK members active in DLP threads — [Disney Pin Forum](https://disneypinforum.com/forums/gatherings-events-meetups.10/); [UK newcomer thread](https://disneypinforum.com/threads/new-to-pin-trading-few-questions-please.39812)

### Inferences
- **ASSUMED** — UK/EU collectors keep their collection and trade state across several places (a Pin & Pop/PinPics catalogue, Instagram DMs, Vinted/eBay listings, Facebook groups). That makes **shareable ISO and For Trade pages** (a public link you can drop into an Instagram bio, Facebook post or Vinted profile) a strong wedge: PinHunt goes where collectors already are instead of asking them to move.
- **ASSUMED** — Because Vinted's protection applies only to "Buy now", and private sales carry no statutory rights, swap-only trades arranged in DMs have no protection at all. That is the trust gap a Trade Circle / trusted-trader reputation could fill without PinHunt handling any payments.

### Gaps
- **No verified member counts** for UK/DLP Facebook pin groups, r/DisneyPins, r/DisneylandParis, or Discord servers. Facebook and Reddit pages did not come up in search, and direct fetching was blocked. Do a manual public check (Facebook group "About" pages show member counts publicly) before sizing.
- No data on the share of UK collectors who use any app at all.

## 3. Competitors: who serves UK/EU well or badly

### Takeaway
PinPal is a US-built (7Labs Cloudware LLC) social marketplace with ISO, trade lists, messaging and a map. I found no evidence of DLP- or UK-specific features, GBP/EUR pricing, or cross-border shipping help. Pin & Pop has the strongest DLP catalogue coverage (5,379 DLP pins) but is a web/PWA without native apps. I found no UK-native app competitor. The UK "competition" is Instagram, Facebook, Vinted and eBay.

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED** — PinPal: Pin Trading & Scanner (7Labs Cloudware LLC) calls itself a "social network and marketplace" for Disney pin collectors to buy, sell, trade, connect and track collections. Features: ISO list, marking pins for trade, browsing other collectors' collections, messaging on a match, and trading spots on a map with "what's nearby" and directions. Its free scanner "assists with identification and does not authenticate physical pins". It is listed on the Austrian App Store, with English (GB) available — [App Store (AT) listing](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)
- **VERIFIED (gap)** — I found nothing showing PinPal country availability, local-currency pricing, DLP/Disney Store UK release tracking or EU shipping features. The search returned "couldn't find any information" — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)
- **VERIFIED** — Pin & Pop: a free Disney pin database (homepage counts range from about 99k to about 118k pins across cached versions) with **5,379 DLP-origin pins**, a DLP Pin Trading Night series page, collection catalogue, Pin Books, an ISO tracker and an online "Trade Arcade" event. - **ASSUMED** — Pin & Pop's DLP depth makes it the natural partner rather than a rival (consistent with the "Pin & Pop integration being explored" note in CLAUDE.md). Mapping PinHunt IDs to Pin & Pop/PinPics IDs would help, because UK/EU listings reference those.
- **ASSUMED** — PinPal's marketplace (buy/sell) positioning and PinHunt's no-payments, trade-first stance target different users. PinHunt can position itself as "trade-first, community-safe, UK/EU-native".

## 4. Trust and safety: scrappers, fakes, scams

### Takeaway
Scrappers and counterfeits are widespread, especially in cheap eBay/Amazon bulk lots, and they have now been confirmed in DLP LE series. Existing apps either disclaim authentication (PinPal) or rely on front/back reference images (Pin & Pop). UK-specific scam data was not found.

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED** — Scrappers are factory rejects, overruns or unauthorised copies, often from the same molds as real pins. Cheap eBay/Amazon bulk packs are "very likely to be scrappers", and many cast-member lanyard pins are scrappers — [Three Kids Three Cats guide (2026)](https://threekidsthreecatsandahusband.com/disney-trading-pins-secrets-scrappers/); [WDWMagic forum](https://forums.wdwmagic.com/threads/disney-pin-trading-fakes-and-scrappers.946919/); [Disney Pin Forum](https://www.disneypinforum.com/threads/scrappers-on-ebay.61591/)
- **VERIFIED** — Ways to spot them: the back stamp/copyright, enamel quality, edges, and seller history — [PinHoarder](https://pinhoarder.com/how-to-spot-fake-disney-pins/); [Disney Pin Forum side-by-side](https://www.disneypinforum.com/threads/side-by-side-examples-real-vs-fake-scrapper.4242/)
- **VERIFIED** — "Scrapper-free" bulk lots on eBay.de alongside "SCRAPPERS 2 lbs" lots show that both kinds circulate in the EU — [eBay.de](https://www.ebay.de/itm/397252356970); [eBay.de](https://www.ebay.de/itm/357486015982)
- **VERIFIED** — Counterfeits of a DLP LE 700 series were confirmed and flagged by Pin & Pop in Feb 2026 — [Disniverse](https://disniverse.com/pintrading/)
- **VERIFIED (anecdotal)** — A DLP visitor lost about €40 on suspected counterfeit pins and switched to official shops only — [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g2079053-i21935-k12469704-Limited_edition_pins_Lineberty-Disneyland_Paris.html)
- **VERIFIED** — The PinPal scanner explicitly "does not authenticate physical pins" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)

### Inferences
- **ASSUMED** — PinHunt's verified Master Catalogue (RLS-gated `verification_status = 'verified'`) can hold **authenticity reference data per pin**: back-stamp photos, known-counterfeit flags and LE edition sizes. Combined with trader reputation (Trade Circle, completed-trade counts, "trusted trader" badges), this is a defensible trust layer that a swap-only community needs more than a payment-protected marketplace does.
- **ASSUMED** — The AI scan should state clearly that it identifies but does not authenticate, as PinPal does, to avoid liability.

### Gaps
- No UK-specific scam statistics (Action Fraud or similar) for collectibles or pin trading.
- No verified Primark, Loungefly or Vinted-specific counterfeit data.

## 5. Regulatory considerations (no payments, Online Safety Act, GDPR for Collectors Nearby)

### Takeaway
User-to-user features (community posts, messaging, trade chat) put PinHunt within the UK Online Safety Act. That means an illegal-harms risk assessment within three months of launch, kept current and documented, with a lighter set of measures if it shows low risk. Collectors Nearby should default to off and approximate (city-level) location, particularly if any users may be under 18, under the ICO Children's Code. Not processing payments keeps PinHunt clear of marketplace and escrow obligations. That point is an inference; I found no source on it.

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED** — All in-scope user-to-user services, whatever their size or location, must carry out a suitable and sufficient illegal-harms risk assessment covering the priority illegal content categories (Ofcom refers to 17 or 18 depending on the document version). It must be done within three months of launch, kept up to date, redone before significant design changes, and recorded in writing — [Ofcom illegal content duties](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/illegal-content-duties-under-the-online-safety-act); [Ofcom OSA Q&A (May 2025)](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/online-safety/information-for-industry/other/online-safety-act-explained-qa-web.pdf?v=409647); [Ofcom risk assessment guidance](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/online-safety/information-for-industry/illegal-harms/risk-assessment-guidance-and-risk-profiles.pdf?v=390984)
- **VERIFIED** — Small services that reasonably assess low risk across all harms face a reduced set of Codes measures. Ofcom has sent formal information requests to small high-risk sites. Fines go up to 10% of turnover or £18m, whichever is greater — [Ofcom OSA Q&A](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/online-safety/information-for-industry/other/online-safety-act-explained-qa-web.pdf?v=409647); [Ofcom enforcement](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/enforcing-the-online-safety-act-scrutinising-illegal-harms-risk-assessments)
- **VERIFIED (per search summary; re-check)** — Ofcom expects providers to publish summaries of their latest risk assessments by November 2026 — [Ofcom illegal content duties](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/illegal-content-duties-under-the-online-safety-act)
- **VERIFIED** — A practical guide for small sites under the OSA — [Russ Garrett](https://russ.garrett.co.uk/2024/12/17/online-safety-act-guide/)
- **VERIFIED** — ICO Children's Code Standard 10: geolocation settings off by default unless there is a compelling reason. More precise location means higher risk. BeReal stopped processing under-18s' precise (street/landmark-level) location and offers only city-level. In Dec 2025 the ICO engaged Snap and Meta over their map features — [ICO strategy update Mar 2025](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/protecting-childrens-privacy-online-our-childrens-code-strategy/children-s-code-strategy-progress-update-march-2025/); [ICO update Dec 2025](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/protecting-childrens-privacy-online-our-childrens-code-strategy/children-s-code-strategy-progress-update-december-2025/); [ICO location settings](https://ico.org.uk/switched-on-to-privacy/choosing-settings/location-settings/)
- **VERIFIED** — The ICO has not published a standalone "location data" guidance document (per a commercial summary) — [Proximate](https://proximateapp.co.uk/guides/ico-guidance-on-location-data/)

### Inferences
- **ASSUMED** — Pin collecting draws families and teenagers (Disney audience), so PinHunt should plan for under-18 users. Its approach should be: Collectors Nearby opt-in, city or outward-postcode granularity only, no live location, and no precise location on profiles. This fits naturally with PinHunt's existing `routes/geocode.ts`, which can coarsen location server-side.
- **ASSUMED** — OSA backlog items: a written illegal-harms risk assessment covering community, messaging and trade chat; reporting and blocking tools; content moderation for posts and DMs; a published summary; and a fraud/scam category covering trade scams. Trade-related fraud is a priority offence area that a trading app will need to address.
- **ASSUMED** — "No payments between collectors; each buys own shipping" avoids payment-services regulation and the trap of looking like you offer buyer protection. It also means PinHunt offers no recourse, so trust features (reputation, verified catalogue, two-sided "posted/received" confirmations) carry more weight. Note that Vinted's protection covers only "Buy now" purchases, which shows users already recognise the difference between protected and unprotected transactions.

### Gaps
- No legal analysis found specifically on whether a no-payment trade-matching app triggers any UK consumer/marketplace obligations (e.g. DMCC Act 2024 fake-review rules for trader reviews). A lawyer should review this.
- EU DSA obligations for EU users (if PinHunt serves DLP collectors in France) were not researched.
- French-language legal/consumer requirements (e.g. Loi Toubon for French-market apps) were not researched.

## Summary: candidate differentiation opportunities (all ASSUMED, ranked by evidence strength)
1. **UK/EU Release & Event Radar** — DLP PTN/Days and Lineberty LE timing, Disney Store UK ticket drops (e.g. 21 Oct 2026, 5pm), and UK meetups (Pinsane, Rochester monthly), linked to ISO alerts. Evidence: strong fragmentation, verified.
2. **Shareable ISO / For Trade pages** for Instagram, Facebook and Vinted bios. Evidence: trading already happens off-platform, verified.
3. **Event trade boards / proxy-pickup matching** for location exclusives and 2-per-guest caps. Evidence: verified caps and exclusives.
4. **Cross-border trade-shipping helper** (CN22 templates, €45 gift threshold, €3/HS + €2 fee estimate, Royal Mail options), keeping to the "each collector buys own shipping" rule. Evidence: verified fee change 1 Jul 2026.
5. **GBP/EUR valuation** from eBay UK/DE/FR plus Vinted. Evidence: US apps are USD-centric (inferred); EU resale is active (verified).
6. **Trust layer**: verified catalogue with back-stamp references and counterfeit flags, plus Trade Circle reputation. Evidence: verified counterfeit problem; competitors disclaim authentication.
7. **Compliance as a feature**: privacy-safe, city-level Collectors Nearby and an OSA-ready moderation and reporting setup, which give parents and the community reasons to trust the app.
