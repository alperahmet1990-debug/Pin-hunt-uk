# PinPal (thepinpal.com): User Feedback Research

Research date: 2026-10-14. Researcher: Claude (subagent).

**Method and access limits (read first).** In this environment, the network proxy blocked direct fetches of thepinpal.com, apps.apple.com, itunes.apple.com and appgoblin.info. Each request returned "CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403". As a result, everything below comes from **web-search result snippets and summaries**. I could not open any of these pages directly. Labels used:
- **SEEN-IN-SEARCH**: the claim appeared in a search-engine snippet or summary of the named page. I saw the snippet, not the full page.
- **SECOND-HAND**: a third-party aggregator reported the claim, and it is not confirmed against a primary source.
- I could not label anything **VERIFIED (page opened)**, because no primary page could be loaded.

**Bottom line: I found almost no public user feedback about PinPal.** The app appears to be a recent, low-volume iOS release. Its App Store listing has too few ratings to show a score, and I found no Reddit threads, Trustpilot page, Google Play listing, YouTube reviews or forum threads that discuss it. Searching for "PinPal" is also heavily polluted by unrelated products with the same name: a bowling scorekeeper app (id321817464 / PinPal Lite), a sewing-pin magnet, a PinFolio "PinPal" bag clip, the pin-pal.com pin shop, and an old adweek "PinPal" item.

## 1. App Store (UK/US) and Google Play ratings, review counts, recurring themes, developer responses

### Takeaway
PinPal: Pin Trading & Scanner (App Store id6756037215) **had no displayable rating** when it was indexed. Apple shows that state when an app "hasn't received enough ratings or reviews". I found no Google Play listing. There are no review themes or developer responses to analyse.

### Cited Findings
- App identity: "PinPal: Pin Trading & Scanner", publisher **7Labs Cloudware LLC**. Listed as Free with In-App Purchases, rated 13+, in the Lifestyle category, 92.1 MB. SEEN-IN-SEARCH. Source: [App Store (AT storefront, en-GB)](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)
- Ratings: the listing "has no rating score yet" and has not received enough ratings or reviews to display an overview. SEEN-IN-SEARCH. Source: [App Store AT/en-GB](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)
- Pricing: the listing shows a **"Premium" in-app purchase at €9.99** (AT storefront). The listing also advertises "UNLIMITED FREE PIN SCANS". I did not see the GBP or USD price or the billing period. SEEN-IN-SEARCH. Source: [App Store AT/en-GB](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)
- Version history: v1.2.1, dated 2 September (about 35 days before indexing), "improved marketplace and cart layouts and tablet support". SEEN-IN-SEARCH. Source: [App Store AT/en-GB](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)
- The developer's privacy practices "have not been verified by Apple" (the standard App Store note). SEEN-IN-SEARCH. Source: [App Store AT/en-GB](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)
- Disclaimer in the listing: visual search "assists with identification and does not authenticate physical pins". The listing also says users can see what is still missing from sets. SEEN-IN-SEARCH. Source: [App Store AT/en-GB](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)
- AppGoblin lists the same app ID under the older or alternate title **"PinPal: Scan & Organize Pins"**. It names the developer as **Felipe Tavares**, gives a **release in July 2026**, and shows **no ratings yet**. It also lists a 150,000+ pin catalogue claim, a social feed, a buy/sell/trade marketplace with "secure payments and buyer protection", and a map of trading spots. SECOND-HAND (aggregator). Sources: [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6756037215). The developer name conflicts with 7Labs Cloudware LLC on the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB).
- Google Play: searches found no PinPal listing. They only returned other apps: Pin Trading - Scan & Collect, Pin Value, ParkBound/Pinsdex and Disney Pinnacle. Source: [search results incl. Google Play Pin Trading - Scan & Collect](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pintrading.pinscanner&hl=en_US)
- I could not reach the UK (gb) or US (us) storefront pages directly. The only storefront that surfaced in search was Austria (AT, en-GB). A US-storefront ratings page for this app ID did not appear in results.

### Inferences
- The 7Labs Cloudware LLC vs Felipe Tavares mismatch most likely means one of three things: an individual developer account was moved to an LLC, AppGoblin cached older seller data, or Felipe Tavares is the person behind 7Labs Cloudware. Confidence is low.
- The 150,000+ catalogue figure from AppGoblin matches the claim made by the unrelated competitor "Pin Trading - Scan & Collect" (Evergreen Apps). Either the figure is a generic marketing number, or the aggregator mixed up the two apps. Treat it with caution.
- With no displayable rating about 3 months after a July 2026 release, PinPal's install base or its rate of review prompts is very small. It is not yet a meaningful source of user sentiment.
- The listing promises "secure payments and buyer protection" in its marketplace (per AppGoblin). If accurate, PinPal does process payments between collectors. This differs from PinHunt's stance of no marketplace payments or escrow.

### Gaps
- Exact UK and US rating counts, any written reviews, and developer responses. The App Store could not be fetched, and search showed no reviews.
- GBP/USD Premium price and billing period (monthly, annual or lifetime).
- Whether an Android or Google Play version exists. None was found.

## 3. Specific complaints: missing pins (DLP / Disney Store UK / Loungefly), wrong IDs, duplicates, wrong values, subscriptions

### Takeaway
**I found no user reports specific to PinPal** in any of these categories. The only nearby evidence is the category-wide pattern seen in competitor reviews: paywalls and scan limits.

### Cited Findings
- No PinPal-specific reports of missing DLP, Disney Store UK or Loungefly pins, wrong identifications, duplicates, wrong values or subscription problems turned up in any search (see searches above).
- Context only, for a **different app**: reviewers of Pin Trading - Scan & Collect (Evergreen Apps) complain that the free tier caps you at "about 30 pins", and that the app "told me I had to upgrade ($40 a year) after 20 scans". That app shows about 4.5★ from about 1.7K App Store ratings, with reviews that mention scan limits and crashes. SEEN-IN-SEARCH. Sources: [Google Play, Pin Trading - Scan & Collect](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pintrading.pinscanner&hl=en_US), [App Store reviews, Pin Trading - Scan & Collect](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pin-trading-scan-collect/id6751493504?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)
- Context only, competitor conduct: in September 2026, WDW News Today reported that a Disney pin trading app had used AI to alter a woman's appearance in an ad without permission and had repeatedly taken other people's photos and stories. Search context links this to Pin Trading - Scan & Collect, **not PinPal**. Source: [WDWNT, Sept 2026](https://wdwnt.com/2026/09/disney-pin-trading-app-uses-ai-to-alter-womans-appearance-without-permission-for-ad-constantly-stealing-photos-stories-from-others/)
- Possible PinPal-related refund policy: a "PinPal" refund page for a Premium subscription exists at pinpalbot.vercel.app. **I could not tell which PinPal product it belongs to.** It may be a different "PinPal" bot. Source: [pinpalbot.vercel.app/refund](https://pinpalbot.vercel.app/refund)

### Inferences
- PinPal markets "unlimited free scans", which suggests it is positioning against competitors' scan caps, the main paywall complaint in this category. Its €9.99 Premium therefore probably gates something other than scanning, perhaps marketplace or collection features. This is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- All the specific complaint categories remain unanswered for PinPal, because no feedback exists in indexed public sources.

## 5. Overall sentiment trend through 2026-10-14

### Takeaway
I could not establish any trend. PinPal launched around July 2026 (per AppGoblin) and was still being updated in September 2026 (v1.2.1). As of mid-October 2026 it has no measurable public user sentiment.

### Cited Findings
- July 2026 release. SECOND-HAND. Source: [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6756037215)
- v1.2.1 update on 2 September with marketplace/cart layout and tablet fixes. SEEN-IN-SEARCH. Source: [App Store AT/en-GB](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/pinpal-pin-trading-scanner/id6756037215?l=en-GB)

### Inferences
- PinPal is an early-stage entrant. The bigger sources of user sentiment, and the bigger competitive threats, are the established or more-reviewed apps: Pin Trading - Scan & Collect (about 1.7K ratings), MagicPin (about 1.2K ratings at 4.6★ per aggregator [AppFollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/magicpin-disney-pin-collector/1554341677?country=us)), ParkBound/Pinsdex ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pinsdex.app&hl=en_US)) and Collectible PinTrader.
- I found no UK or European user feedback at all. The only European signal is that the app is listed in a European storefront (Austria) with EUR pricing.

### Gaps
- Re-run this research once direct App Store access is available, ideally using the iTunes lookup and customer-reviews RSS for the gb and us storefronts on id6756037215, or after several more months of user adoption.
